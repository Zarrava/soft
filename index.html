About Soft

We believe great interiors start with how you live.

Soft is a boutique interior decor studio built on the idea that a home should reflect its people. We blend timeless aesthetics with thoughtful functionality — creating spaces that are beautiful, livable and uniquely yours.

From a single room refresh to a full home transformation, our team guides you through every step: concept, mood boards, material selection, sourcing and final styling.

  • ✓ Personalized design consultations
  • ✓ Curated material & furniture sourcing
  • ✓ End-to-end project management
  • ✓ Sustainable & local-first choices
